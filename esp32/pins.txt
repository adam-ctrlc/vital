VITAL wiring (ESP32 DevKit 38-pin on a screw-terminal board)

Part             Pin      Terminal  Notes
PZEM-004T TX     GPIO16   P16       UART2 RX
PZEM-004T RX     GPIO17   P17       UART2 TX
PZEM-004T VCC    3V3                3.3 V keeps its TX inside the ESP32's input range
DS18B20 data     GPIO32   P32       red to 3V3, black to GND
LCD SDA / SCL    GPIO13 / GPIO14    P13 / P14, VCC to 5V
Relay IN         GPIO18   P18       through an NPN (see below), module VCC to 5V

Relay: P18 -> 4.7k -> NPN base (2N2222/BC547), collector -> module IN, emitter -> GND.
  A 3.3 V pin cannot switch this 5 V module off by itself. Set RELAY_VIA_TRANSISTOR in
  src/Config.h to match (1 with the NPN, 0 if IN is wired straight to P18).
  Load: live through COM and NO, so an unpowered or booting board leaves it off.

Avoid: GPIO6-11 (flash), GPIO0/2/5/12/15 (boot strapping), GPIO1/3 (USB serial),
GPIO34-39 (input only). ADC2 pins do not read analog while Wi-Fi is on.

Hardware fixes for resets and Wi-Fi drops (firmware cannot fix these):
- Power the board from a solid 5 V 2 A supply, not a laptop USB port, and put a
  470-1000 uF capacitor across 5V and GND near the ESP32. Wi-Fi bursts and the relay
  coil share that rail; a dip resets the board (it then reports "brownout").
- Put an RC snubber (or a MOV) across the relay contacts. Switching the transformer
  makes a spark whose noise can reset the ESP32 or drop Wi-Fi.
- Keep the mains wiring away from the ESP32, LCD and sensor wires.
- Add a 4.7k resistor from DS18B20 data to 3V3; the internal pull-up is too weak for
  long wires.
- If the LCD backpack has pull-ups to 5V on SDA/SCL, remove them or use a level shifter.
