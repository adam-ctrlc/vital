VITAL firmware

esp32.ino            setup() and loop(): starts the protection task and the uplink.
secrets.example.h    copy to secrets.h (gitignored) with Wi-Fi and API details.
pins.txt             wiring and hardware notes.

src/Config.h         pins, relay polarity, timings.
src/core/
  Protection         the overload logic: alarm, trip (VA or 9.09 A, after the delay),
                     instant trip at 90.91 A, reclose, lockout. Pure C++.
  Controller         runs Protection on its own FreeRTOS task: sensors, relay, LCD, flash.
  Settings           limits, delays and trip state saved in flash (Preferences).
  Boot               reset reason, brownout count, last relay command id (RTC memory).
src/hardware/        Sensors (PZEM-004T + DS18B20), Relay, Lcd.
src/net/
  Uplink             loop(): posts readings every 5 s, a heartbeat every 30 s.
  Backend            HTTPS to the API, relay command ids acknowledged.
  Wifi               non-blocking; the driver reconnects, this only covers it giving up.
  LiveServer         http://<board>/live for the app on the same network.

tests/
  protection/        ./run.sh runs the protection logic on a computer.
  relay, pzem, lcd, ds18b20, net
                     one sketch per part, for checking the wiring. Open the folder as a
                     sketch and upload it; flash esp32/ again afterwards.

Libraries: ArduinoJson, PZEM004Tv30, OneWire, DallasTemperature, hd44780,
ESP Async WebServer (+ Async TCP). Board: esp32 by Espressif, 3.x.
